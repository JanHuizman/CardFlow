Features

Everything you need, nothing you don't

Designed around a single workflow — get data off cards and into folders, fast.

Up to 16 cards simultaneously

Connect as many SD cards as your hub supports. Each slot copies independently — no waiting for one to finish before the next starts.

Named destination folders

Give each card its own folder name before copying. Your data lands exactly where you want it, already organised.

Auto-detects new cards

Insert a card and CardFlow detects it within 2 seconds, assigns it to the next empty slot automatically.

Safe re-runs — no duplicates

Files already at the destination are skipped. Run CardFlow again without creating duplicates or overwriting data.

Clean & eject after copy

Once a perfect copy is confirmed, CardFlow wipes and safely ejects all cards with a two-step confirmation.

All file types, all devices

Works with any device that stores files on an SD card — cameras, drones, recorders, sensors. Preserves folder structure.

Workflow

Four steps. That's it.

CardFlow is designed to get out of your way as quickly as possible.

1

Insert your cards

CardFlow detects each card automatically and assigns it to a slot.

2

Name the folders

Type a destination folder name for each card — or leave it to use the drive letter.

3

Choose output & copy

Select your output folder and press Copy. Progress is shown per slot in real time.

4

Done

Files are organised and ready. Optionally wipe and eject all cards in one click.
